What this project practises
  • Binary search for boundaries (1.5.1): counting in a range in O(log n), whatever the range.
  • A sliding window driven by two pointers (1.5.2, 1.5.3) that only move forward.
  • Choosing by size: 50 000 queries × 500 000 detections is too much for a scan per query, and fine for two binary searches per query.

The situation

A meteor camera records the time of every meteor it detects, all night, every night of a shower such as the Perseids. Three questions come back: how many meteors between two moments (asked again and again while exploring the data), the peak (the busiest window), and the periods with no detection at all, which are suspicious: a cloud, or the camera, can hide meteors as well as an empty sky.
