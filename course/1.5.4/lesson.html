What this project practises
  • Binary search for boundaries (1.5.1): counting in a range in O(log n), whatever the range.
  • A sliding window driven by two pointers (1.5.2, 1.5.3) that only move forward.
  • Choosing by size: 50 000 queries × 500 000 errors is too much for a scan per query, and fine for two binary searches per query.

The situation

After an incident you have the timestamps of every error, sorted. The post-mortem needs three numbers: how many errors between two moments (asked again and again while you explore), the worst minute, and the periods with no errors at all, which are suspicious: a dead service produces no errors either.
